# Software and Acknowledgments

**Software.** This work used Python 3.13 with NumPy, SciPy and pandas for the Monte Carlo simulation; SALib for the Sobol and Latin hypercube sensitivity analysis; Matplotlib for the figures; Astropy for the machine-readable table; and PyArrow for the Parquet snapshot. Exact versions are listed in `requirements.txt`. The certified run was executed on Google Colab (CPU runtime). The planet catalog is a dated snapshot (2026-11-18) of the NASA Exoplanet Archive Planetary Systems Composite Parameters table (`pscomppars`), queried through its TAP service. The benchmark used the Habitable Worlds Catalog of the Planetary Habitability Laboratory, University of Puerto Rico at Arecibo (file last updated 2024-03-12). The PHL catalog is downloaded at run time and is not redistributed with this package.

**Use of AI agents.** AI agents with computing tools assisted in this work. A coding/execution agent implemented the model specification in Python, ran the simulations and sensitivity analyses on local and cloud (Google Colab) runtimes, generated tables and figures from scripts, and checked the cited literature values. A second AI agent, acting as a technical consultant ("R-Aetherios"), audited the code and methodology and proposed corrections. Every change proposed by the agents was reviewed and approved by the lead author before it was adopted, and all code, data and outputs are released in full for independent verification.

**Authorship and responsibility.** The scientific concept, the definition of the Unified Habitability Index (UHI), the choice of assumptions and thresholds, the interpretation of the results and the conclusions belong entirely to the lead author, Effy Viridis. She bears full intellectual authorship of and scientific responsibility for this work. The AI agents are tools and are not authors.

This research has made use of the NASA Exoplanet Archive, which is operated by the California Institute of Technology under contract with the National Aeronautics and Space Administration under the Exoplanet Exploration Program.
